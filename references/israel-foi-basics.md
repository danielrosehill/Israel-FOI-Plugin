# Israeli FOI basics (חוק חופש המידע, התשנ"ח-1998)

Shared by every skill in this plugin. Marked CONFIRMED where a source was read,
INFERRED where it is reasoning. Last reviewed 2026-11-23.

## Who to ask

- A request goes to the **public authority that holds the information**, through
  that authority's own FOI officer (הממונה על העמדת מידע לציבור). There is **no
  central office** for municipal requests: the national FOI unit and ministries
  refer municipal questions back to the municipality's own officer. (CONFIRMED:
  gov.il FOI unit pages; a ministry answer of May 2026 redirecting to the municipal officer.)
- **Building permits, permit files and their conditions** are held by the **local
  planning and building committee / licensing authority**, not by the district
  committee or the Planning Administration. The national bodies handle *plans*
  (תב"ע), not permit files, and decline documents "created in another authority".
  (INFERRED from how permits are issued, plus Planning Administration FOI decisions.)
- In cities where the local committee works inside the municipality and has
  no FOI officer of its own (Jerusalem: none found), file with the
  **municipality's FOI officer** and **name the committee and licensing authority
  as the holder** in the request text.
- Nuisance enforcement (noise, dust, working hours under a municipal by-law),
  complaints and inspections belong to the municipality's environment and
  inspection units, so the municipality is the right authority for those too.

## The s.20 caveat: ask for direct inspection as well

FOI s.20: where another law provides its own access route, that route takes
precedence. The Planning Administration has applied this by sending requesters
to the Planning & Building Law's inspection route (s.96א) instead of the FOI
route (decision of 3.12.2024). **A permit file request may be answered with
"come and inspect the building file".** So, alongside the FOI request:

- request direct inspection of the building file (עיון בתיק בניין), or search it
  on the municipality's planning site, which often lists permit files by
  address, gush/helka or plan;
- keep in the FOI request whatever has no other access route: complaints,
  inspections, enforcement, correspondence, monitoring reports, internal schedules.

## Clock and fees

- Answer within **30 days** of receipt; extendable by 30 days with notice, and
  longer only with written reasons (s.7). (Commonly cited; check the current text.)
- Fees: a **request fee** (24 ₪ on gov.il as of 2026-11; older pages say 20 ₪) plus
  a **processing/production fee**. Forms ask you to commit to a cap (Jerusalem: 178 ₪
  including the request fee) and must ask before going over it.
  Requests about yourself are partly exempt.
- Refusals can be challenged in the administrative court within 45 days.

## Writing the request

- Hebrew. Name the holder unit(s). Identify the file precisely: building file no.,
  permit no. and date, gush/helka, address.
- Numbered items, each a **document or a fact the authority holds** (copies,
  counts, dates), not a question asking for an opinion.
- Ask that third-party identifiers be redacted rather than the item withheld.
- Ask to be told what is held elsewhere and by whom (s.8(4)).
- Ask for advance notice before any fee above the request fee.
- Ask for digital delivery by email.
- Keep a full version as a PDF. Online forms cap the free-text field (Jerusalem: 400
  chars), so the form gets a summary and the PDF is attached.
